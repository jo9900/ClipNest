# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS

## Stack

Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit. Native application requested by the owner.

## Users and purpose

A personal clipboard utility for quickly recovering something copied earlier.
The owner also wants a public GitHub repository under jo9900 and a free Mac App Store app.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Centered, fixed history panel; select an entry to copy it and dismiss; Escape dismisses.
- Keep the latest 20 entries, evict the oldest, and clear all immediately without a confirmation.
- Rows show content and type; no ticking timestamp or countdown.
- Optional launch at login. Keep source files small and responsibilities separate.
- Confirmed name and content scope: Copiary Clipboard (formerly ClipNest; renamed November 2026 because of an EU trademark conflict), text and links. Do not reintroduce "ClipNest" in any user-facing text or store metadata.
- Follow macOS language preferences, with 18 interface localizations and Arabic RTL layout.
- Implementation defaults: local persistent history, macOS 13 and later,
  Intel and Apple silicon.
- Store publication requires the owner's Apple Developer team and distribution access.

## Brand commitments

An English product name, a simple tasteful icon, and a quiet native macOS interface.
