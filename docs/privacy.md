# ClipNest Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

ClipNest is a local clipboard history utility maintained by jo9900.

## Data on your Mac

After you enable history, ClipNest reads new text and links from the system
clipboard while running. It stores at most 20 entries with a random identifier and
copy time in its sandbox's Application Support/ClipNest/history.json file. It stores
your recording and pause choices in the app's preferences. Launch at login is managed
by macOS. History is local JSON and is not separately encrypted by the app.

ClipNest makes no network requests and has no network entitlement. It has no account,
advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry, or cloud synchronization. Clipboard
contents and settings are not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

## Your controls

Pause recording from settings or the menu bar. Delete an individual clip from its
context menu, or choose Clear All to delete the local history file. Clear All does
not clear the current system clipboard or copies that may exist in your Mac backups.
Quit ClipNest to stop reading the clipboard. Disable Launch at login to stop automatic
startup. You can also revoke clipboard access in macOS settings where available.

ClipNest skips contents with concealed, transient, auto-generated, or supported
password-manager markers. It cannot detect all sensitive content; ordinary unmarked
passwords or other private text may be stored. Pause recording before copying them.

## Contact

For questions, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/jo9900/ClipNest/issues).
Do not include your clipboard history or other private information in public issues.
